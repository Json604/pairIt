linux

Choose your processor: Intel/AMD x64 for most PCs, or ARM64 for ARM computers. Both installers below are for Ubuntu / Debian. The website does not automatically detect your processor.

Intel/AMD x64 · .debARM64 · .deb

Preview · Ubuntu 24.04 / Debian 12 or newer · X11 or XWayland · PipeWire/PulseAudio

On Ubuntu or Debian, download the .deb for your computer, open it with the system package installer, and choose Install. It installs the required libraries and adds PairIt to your application menu. No separate .NET installation is needed.

The tray menu provides Open PairIt, Settings and Quit on desktops with AppIndicator/StatusNotifier support. GNOME may require its AppIndicator extension. Without tray support, PairIt remains accessible as a normal application window; minimize it to keep sharing.

Other compatible distributions: portable x64 archive · portable ARM64 archive. These still require X11/XWayland and PulseAudio-compatible client tools; follow START-HERE.txt.

Select the output your apps use, start sharing, and pair your phone. Use the current phone app; iPhone availability depends on TestFlight or App Store approval. The desktop confirms pairing and lets you switch between computer speakers and phone headphones without losing connections.

Phone playback uses a temporary private output so the selected laptop speakers do not also play. Computer or final disconnect restores local playback; pausing holds the private output. Optional phone microphone forwarding uses a separately selected virtual sink. No automatic player pause or video compensation. Ubuntu PulseAudio/PipeWire and Debian tests passed; physical Linux/Bluetooth testing remains necessary.

updates and source

On desktop build 22 or newer, choose Download & install in PairIt’s update notice or Settings. PairIt downloads and verifies the matching update, then closes and reopens. You can cancel the download. Installing stops sharing; saved pairing and settings stay in your user profile.

Mac installs signed, Apple-notarized updates. Windows and portable Linux apps need their complete app folder in a writable location. Ubuntu/Debian installation asks for system authorization. Install build 22 or newer once to enable in-app updates.

Phone store installations continue updating through their store. Release notes and previous downloads.
